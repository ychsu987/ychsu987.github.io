Teaching activity

Teaching activity

  • I draw many illustrations and mathematical examples during class.
  • Illustrations might not be uploaded.
  • From an educational psychology perspective, writing things down helps you learn more than typing them.
  • The class pace and difficulty are adjustable.

Tutorial questions

  • Each class consists of lectures and tutorial questions related to the topics.
  • I won’t cover all tutorial questions.
  • Exam questions are simple modifications of tutorial questions.

In-class Quiz

  • You need to record a video
    • Content: how do you solve a tutorial question
    • Deadline: Monday or Thursday noon
    • Submission methods: Class forum
  • Quiz questions
    • Voting a question (with video recording)/class concept to let Ray do it
  • Complete either submissions (i.e. video or quiz) to get full marks
  • You will only see answers for tutorial questions with video submission.
  • Complete both submissions (i.e., video and quiz) to get full marks.

Correctness of the video is not an evaluation criterion
